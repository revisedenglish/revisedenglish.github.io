<h1>Welcome to Revised English</h1>

English is one of the most widely spoken languages in the world—but also one of the hardest to spell. Unlike many other languages, English spelling doesn’t always match how words are pronounced. This creates confusion for learners, wastes time in education, and leads to countless mistakes even among native speakers.

Revised English is a modern spelling reform plan designed to make English easier to read, write, and learn. It keeps the richness of the language while removing unnecessary complications. The system is simple, logical, and consistent: words are written closer to the way they are spoken.

Our goal is not to replace English, but to improve it. Revised English offers a clear path toward a more accessible language—one that saves time in education, supports literacy worldwide, and helps English continue to thrive as the international language of communication.
