<h1>Accent</h1>

<h2>Neutral Spelling Dialect</h2>

Revised English is designed to reflect a roughly neutral hybrid of the national accents of the United States (General American) and Great Britain (Received Pronunciation), the two largest and the most influential dialects of English. Essentially, this combines the British vowel inventory with American rhoticity.

The goal is to create a spelling accent which is based on a common model for non-natives which deviates little from every major dialect as to render transnational communication easy and effective. Another aim is to provide a standard basis for more phonetic spelling so that such communication remains equally efficient in written form.
